01Call for papers

A national forum for IT and intelligent systems

NCITIS’26 is organized by the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics at the University of Batna 2, Algeria, in collaboration with two research laboratories of the University.

The conference aims to create a forum for researchers, faculty members and industry specialists to share insights, exchange ideas and build foundations for collaboration on recent advances and evolving trends in computer science, information technology, intelligent systems and artificial intelligence.

02Main topics

Four conference axes

Contributions are invited on, but not limited to, the following tracks.

01
Axis 1

Artificial Intelligence and Big Data

Advanced AI methodologies, including machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and data-driven insights across industries, together with frameworks, algorithms and systems for big data analytics and intelligent systems.

  • Machine learning and deep learning
  • Generative AI and data-driven insights
  • Big data analytics and intelligent systems
02
Axis 2

Human-Centric and Emerging Technologies

Sustainable IT solutions, user experience design and emerging technologies with a social impact, along with the ethical concerns that are crucial for advancing responsible technologies.

  • Sustainable IT and user experience
  • Emerging technologies and social impact
  • Ethical and responsible technologies
03
Axis 3

Security Strategies for IoT, Cloud and Edge Computing

Innovative strategies, methodologies and solutions for security in interconnected and distributed environments, aiming to secure modern infrastructures and enable secure real-time data processing.

  • Cryptography and data integrity
  • Secure communication protocols
  • Cyber-threat detection and mitigation
04
Axis 4

Distributed Systems and Blockchain

Advances in distributed computing and blockchain technologies, with an emphasis on reliability, scalability and innovative applications in healthcare, supply chain and other domains. Use cases and theoretical advances are highly encouraged.

  • Reliability and scalability
  • Blockchain applications and innovation

04Submission

Submit a short paper

Short papers take the form of a 2-page extended abstract (about 800–1,200 words), written with the official NCITIS’26 template and submitted through Microsoft CMT.

Step 1 · Prepare

Extended abstract template

Use the official LaTeX template to write your 2-page extended abstract (about 800–1,200 words), keeping its layout. The bibliography is compiled with biber.

Download (.zip)
“Open in Overleaf” creates your own copy of the template in your Overleaf account. Preview the template (PDF)
Step 2 · Submit

Submission page on Microsoft CMT

Sign in to Microsoft CMT (or create a free account), open the NCITIS2026 conference and upload your paper before 25 November 2026.

cmt3.research.microsoft.com Go to the submission page

How it works

  1. Choose an axis. Check that your work fits one of the four conference axes.
  2. Use the template. Write the 2-page extended abstract with the official template, in Overleaf or from the .zip.
  3. Submit on CMT. Upload the paper on Microsoft CMT before 25 November 2026.
  4. Register. After acceptance (01 December), register by 02–03 December 2026.
